Professional inquiry privacy notice

How inquiry information is handled.

This notice applies to information voluntarily submitted through the professional inquiry form on this portfolio.

Information collected

The form may collect your name, email address, organization, role, professional profile, website, location or time zone, inquiry type, expected timeline, discovery source, and message.

Purpose of collection

The information is used only to assess, organize, and respond to legitimate professional inquiries, opportunities, collaborations, or requests.

Storage and service provider

Submissions may be processed and stored by the form service configured for this site, such as Formspree. Review that provider's terms and privacy practices before activating the form.

Retention

Information may be retained while an inquiry is active or where reasonably needed for professional recordkeeping. Irrelevant, abusive, or outdated submissions may be deleted.

Sharing

Inquiry information is not intended for sale or public distribution. It may be disclosed when required by law, to address abuse, or to operate the form service.

Your responsibility

Do not submit passwords, identification numbers, financial data, medical information, confidential business information, proprietary documents, or other sensitive material.

No relationship createdSubmitting an inquiry does not create an employment, consulting, contractual, fiduciary, or other professional relationship.
