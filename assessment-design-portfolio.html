SELECTED WORK

Eight assessment design case studies

Each case study is self-contained and portfolio-ready. Secure item content, answer keys, internal IDs, URLs, and respondent-level data are intentionally withheld.

METHOD

A repeatable assessment-development system

The work follows a traceable chain from construct definition to evidence collection and post-launch quality monitoring.

01

Define the construct

Clarify what competent performance means and where simple recall would produce weak evidence.

02

Build the skills architecture

Decompose broad capabilities into clusters and observable skills that can be assessed directly.

03

Blueprint the evidence

Map skills to contexts, challenge structure, interaction types, and intended cognitive demand.

04

Design the experience

Use realistic scenarios and varied interactions to elicit judgment, application, analysis, and explanation.

05

Review for quality

Check construct coverage, clarity, accessibility, bias risk, scoring logic, and consistency.

06

Monitor and iterate

Use performance diagnostics, feedback, and review signals to identify items needing attention without exposing secure content.
